Process
The inspection

What happens when we come out

Ten to fifteen minutes on site, and you get it in writing either way.

  1. 01

    Drone survey

    Three passes around the roof: the field, then the details, then soft metals, gutters, screens and fencing.

  2. 02

    On the roof if needed

    Where the imagery cannot confirm damage or find a leak, the inspector gets on the roof and into the attic.

  3. 03

    Talked through on site

    You hear the findings while the evidence is fresh and can ask questions there and then.

  4. 04

    Report by email

    A written record of the roof condition, whether or not there is anything to do about it.
