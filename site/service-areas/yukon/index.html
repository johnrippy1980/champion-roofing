Yukon questions

Roofing in Yukon, answered

The practical questions people in this part of the metro actually ask.

Why does metal come up so much in Yukon?

More shops, outbuildings and properties with long simple roof runs than in some parts of the metro. Metal suits that kind of building well: it sheds water fast and handles wind properly when it is fastened correctly.

What decides whether a metal roof lasts?

The detailing, not the panel. Panel layout, allowance for thermal movement and the flashing at every transition get planned before anything is ordered, not resolved on the roof.

Do you still do ordinary shingle roofs in Yukon?

Of course. Full residential roofing, repair, replacement and inspection, same as anywhere on our list. Metal is simply the thing that comes up here more often.

Will you tell me if metal is wrong for my roof?

Yes. There are roofs where it is the wrong answer on cost, on look or on the building, and we would rather say so than sell it.
