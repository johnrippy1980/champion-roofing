Why there is no page here for every city

A common tactic in local search is to create dozens of near identical pages, each swapping in a different city name, in the hope of ranking for all of them. You have probably read a few without realising it.

We are not doing that. It produces pages that are useless to read, and search engines have been treating them as what they are for a long time now. This single honest page covers where we work.

  • Nine cities, listed plainly. Rather than nine near identical pages.
  • One real address. 7608 N Council Rd, Oklahoma City, OK 73132.
  • Same pricing across the metro. No travel surcharge appearing later.
  • An honest answer if you are outside it. Including a pointer somewhere else when we are not the right fit.

Questions about where we work

Including why you will not find a page here for every suburb.

Which cities does Champion Roofing serve?

Oklahoma City, Edmond, Norman, Moore, Midwest City, Del City, Yukon, Mustang and Bethany.

We operate from 7608 N Council Rd in northwest Oklahoma City, so the whole metro is within a reasonable working radius.

What if I am just outside your service area?

Call the office anyway. We would rather tell you honestly that we are not the right fit than have you guessing, and if we cannot help we will usually be able to point you toward someone who can.

What we will not do is stretch a crew somewhere we cannot properly look after you afterwards, because the warranty means nothing if we cannot get back to you.

Do you charge more to travel across the metro?

No. The cities listed here are all within our normal working area and are priced the same way.

If a property is far enough out that travel genuinely changes the job, we will tell you that up front rather than adding it later. The quoted price is the price.

Do you have separate pages for each city?

Deliberately not. Thin pages that swap one city name for another are a well known search engine tactic and they are of no use to you as a homeowner.
